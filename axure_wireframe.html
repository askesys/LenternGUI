Omelette

640 kcal 4 portions 15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 pinch pepper
  • 1 tbsp butter

Steps

1. Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt and pepper.
2. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in the egg mixture and let it set on the bottom.
4. Fold in half and slide onto a plate. Serve hot.
